WashPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · WashPro, Hyderabad, India · Contact: support@washpro.in

1. What we collect

Account data: name, mobile number, email address (when you sign in with Phone, Email or Google via Firebase Authentication). Vehicles: nickname, make, model, colour and registration number you add. Addresses: service address and parking details (tower, basement slot, gate notes) needed to reach your car. Bookings: services booked, schedule, price paid, before/after service photos and ratings. Location: during an active job, our washing expert shares live location so you can track arrival; we do not track your personal location. Device data: basic app/device identifiers used for crash reporting and security.

2. How we use it

To create and service your bookings and subscriptions, assign a washing expert, process payments and refunds, send booking updates (SMS/push), provide support, prevent fraud and improve our service. We never sell your personal data.

3. Sharing

Your assigned washing expert sees your first name, vehicle details and service address/parking notes for the job. Payments are processed by our payment partner (Razorpay); we do not store card or UPI credentials. We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, database and hosting under their security standards. We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Data retention & security

Booking and payment records are kept for accounting/legal purposes (typically 8 years for invoices); other profile data is kept while your account is active. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and access-controlled with Firebase Security Rules so users can only access their own data.

5. Your rights

You can view, edit or delete vehicles and addresses in the app anytime. For account deletion or data export, use Profile → Delete account or write to support@washpro.in; we action requests within 30 days, subject to legal retention duties.

6. Children

WashPro is for users 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
