Delete your WashPro account

You can delete your WashPro account and personal data at any time, in either of these ways:

Option 1 — In the app (instant):
Open WashPro → Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your profile, vehicles, addresses, subscriptions and notifications are erased and your sign-in is removed immediately.

Option 2 — By email:
Email support@washproo.in from your registered email address with the subject “Delete my account”. We verify and complete deletion within 30 days, and confirm by email.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: name, email, phone, saved vehicles, saved addresses, subscriptions, notifications, support tickets.
  • Kept (anonymised): booking and payment/invoice records with your name and contact removed — retained only as required by Indian tax and accounting law.

Questions? Contact support@washproo.in. See also our Privacy Policy.
